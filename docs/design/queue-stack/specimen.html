What to look for

  • Does the current readout stay the focus at depth 4? That's the constraint the whole feature has to clear. Push Depth to 4 with Long names on.
  • The token ramp runs out. --paper / --paper-dim / --paper-medium is three tiers; a fourth entry lands near --paper-faint (0.14 alpha), which is decorative, not legible. Compare against Compressed, and check both themes — light mode's dimmed tiers sit on a light ground and fade faster.
  • Long names are the stress case. "B♭ Half-Diminished (m7♭5)" is a real entry. B (Rail) has the least room for it; A and C wrap instead.
  • Narrow. Below 560px .readout-row wraps today. A right-hand column landing under the current readout at full width is a different layout, not a smaller one.
  • Position numerals answer open question 3 — is "how many ahead" worth stating, or does stack position already say it?
