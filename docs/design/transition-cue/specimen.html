Findings that outlived the feature

The darkening was for small label text

theming.md: --brass is the one accent that forks per theme (#ce9b2e → #8a6412) because at its dark-mode value it reads as washed-out, low-contrast text on a light page — “small uppercase labels (TIMING, SOUND, PRESETS…) especially.” That darkening is correct for those labels. It is why the light theme’s gold is a deep olive rather than a gold, and it is exactly what you’re seeing as muddy in the beat track.

A block is a fill; the numeral is text — and that’s the whole problem

A filled 12×18px block has no contrast requirement worth the name, so it could take the bright gold in light mode and look right. The numeral can’t: it’s text. Asking the two to match — which is the right call, they read as one object — means the fill inherits the numeral’s contrast floor. That’s the trade this round makes visible.

The floor is lower than the one that forced the original darkening, though. The numeral is large display type — clamp(2.4rem, 7vw, 3.25rem), so 38–52px in the app — which clears WCAG’s large-text bar at 3:1 rather than the 4.5:1 those small uppercase labels need. That gap is the room to work in:

Note what the table does not say: none of this touches --brass itself. The settings-group legends keep the deep value they need. A second token — off-beat gold, used by the beat track only — is the change, and it only needs a light-mode value, since dark mode was never the problem.

The other reason light mode reads flatter

Dark mode puts a bright gold and a bright flame on a dark panel: both advance, and they differ mostly in hue. Light mode puts two dark-ish colours on white: both recede, and they now differ in lightness as well as hue, so the pair reads as “dark and darker” rather than “two accents.” Bringing the gold up narrows that lightness gap, which is most of what makes it feel like the dark-mode version.

Constraints any future attempt inherits

  • Does brass want this job at all? Flame already means “downbeat accent”; brass means “Seventh Chords, the Next sticker, settings-group legend.” A dedicated off-beat token sidesteps this — it need not be gold.
  • Colour can’t be the only signal. Red/gold is the most common colour-vision confusion there is. The scaleY(1.15) on the current block carries position non-chromatically and covers it — worth not removing.
  • The PiP console has its own beat blocks (.pip-beat--current) and its own numeral. #22’s rule is that the console shows what the display shows.
